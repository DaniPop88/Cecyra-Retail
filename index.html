A shop that knows the neighborhood.

Cecyra is a community-oriented retail shop founded by Ma. Cecilia Talaro Amaba, serving Evergreen Homes and neighboring barangays.

Our mission is to be the neighborhood grocery that families trust for fresh produce, reliable essentials and friendly service. Ma. Cecilia and a dedicated local team manage the shop, ready to assist you quickly and respectfully.

Quick answers

How do I order for delivery?

Fill your bayong and send your order here, or call +63 921 370 8965 or email info@cecyrashop.online with your item list and address.

What payment options do you take?

Cash, GCash, PayMaya, and in-store card payments.

Can I pick up in-store?

Yes. Choose store pickup when ordering and tell us your pickup time.

What about returns?

Perishables: replacement or refund within 24 hours with receipt. Non-perishables: see our refund policy.
